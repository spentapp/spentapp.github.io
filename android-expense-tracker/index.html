Android expense tracking without a bank connection

SPENT helps you record expenses, manage a budget and see your spending grow into a 3D city on Android. No bank connection is required.

Get SPENT on Google Play iPhone version

Getting started on Android

Add a purchase manually with the quick-add screen, select a category and review it in your history. Each expense contributes to a living 3D city representing your spending for the month. If you prefer a more traditional overview, use the budget and spending-history views.

Optional notification-based capture

On supported Android devices, SPENT can help record expenses from payment notifications after you enable the appropriate setup and permissions. This is not a bank feed. Capture depends on the phone, card issuer and payment service, so it may miss transactions. Check your history and add missing purchases manually.

Manage a monthly budget

Track income and spending, create a budget for the month, and compare categories with your plan. The city is a visual summary, while the history shows the underlying purchases. SPENT is a tracking tool and does not display an official bank balance.

Track shared expenses

Couples, roommates or friends can use a shared city to record purchases together and see who paid. Shared cities are separate from personal expenses. Read the shared expenses guide to understand how access, budgets and purchases work. Shared expenses guide.

Your first week with SPENT

Start with a few categories you use regularly, then record purchases and verify the amounts. If you activate notification capture, test a supported payment. At the end of the week, compare your history with actual purchases to catch missed or duplicate records.

Frequently asked questions

Does SPENT connect to my bank on Android?

No. SPENT does not require bank login credentials. Record expenses yourself or use supported payment notifications after setup.

Does every payment appear automatically?

No. Notification capture depends on your device, notification settings and payment provider. Some transactions still require manual entry.

Can Android and iPhone users track shared expenses?

SPENT supports shared cities on supported Android and iPhone devices. Each member can record expenses and see who paid.

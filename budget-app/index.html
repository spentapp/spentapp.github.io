Monthly budget app for iPhone: plan and review your month

A monthly budget app for iPhone and Android in English and Hebrew: track income and expenses, set category budgets and review the month in SPENT.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

A budget you can revisit

SPENT combines expense tracking with a monthly budget, category budgets, income tracking and savings goals. It works in English and Hebrew, and every purchase you record is also tracked against your plan. If you add expenses without linking a bank, see expense tracker for iPhone without a bank connection. Your spending also appears as a 3D city, with different areas representing categories.

Start with your own numbers

Choose your main currency and record the income and expenses you want to track. Set a monthly budget that reflects those records, then decide which categories need their own limits. For example, separate groceries from restaurants if that distinction helps you review purchases. Any numbers you choose are your own plan; SPENT does not promise a particular saving outcome.

Use recurring expenses carefully

Add regular payments as recurring expenses so they are represented in your plan. When reviewing the month, check dates, amounts and categories against actual payments. If you record the same purchase manually and automatically, check the history for duplicates. A tracking app is most useful when the records reflect what actually happened.

Review and adjust

Compare spending by category with the limits you set. Ask which purchases were expected and which were missing from the plan. Adjust next month based on those observations. Savings goals track progress toward a target; they are not a savings account or a way to invest money.

Personal or shared

You can track a personal budget or create a shared city with a partner, roommates or friends. Shared cities require Sign in with Apple (or Google on Android) and internet access to sync. They are useful for purchases that belong in one shared record. Keep personal purchases in your personal space if they do not belong in that shared budget. How to set this up is covered in shared budget app for couples and roommates.

Questions about budgeting in SPENT

Can I set a budget for each category?

Yes. You set a monthly budget and can give individual categories their own limits, then compare spending in each category with its limit.

Does SPENT handle recurring payments?

You can add regular payments as recurring expenses so they appear in your plan. Check dates and amounts against the actual payments when you review the month.

Do savings goals move money?

No. Savings goals track progress toward a target you choose. They are not a savings account and do not invest money.

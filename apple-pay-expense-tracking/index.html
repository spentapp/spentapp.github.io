How to track Apple Pay spending automatically on iPhone

You can track Apple Pay spending automatically on iPhone without linking a bank account. A personal automation in the Shortcuts app sends the amount and merchant of each supported Apple Pay purchase to an expense tracker such as SPENT, which adds the expense for you.

How automatic Apple Pay tracking works

When you pay with Apple Pay, Wallet can show a transaction notification for that card. The Shortcuts app has a Wallet trigger that runs an automation at that moment and hands it the amount and the merchant name. SPENT adds a Shortcuts action called Record Apple Pay Transaction, which turns those two values into an expense in your history and in your city.

No bank connection is involved. SPENT does not ask for bank login details, does not read your bank account and does not get access to your card. It only receives what the automation on your own iPhone passes to it.

What you need

  • iOS 27 or later for the guided setup inside SPENT. On earlier versions there is a manual setup option at the bottom of the capture screen, but transaction details may be incomplete.
  • A card in Apple Wallet whose purchases show a transaction notification. This depends on your card and bank.
  • SPENT, free on the App Store.

Set it up in SPENT, step by step

The setup takes a few minutes and is done once. SPENT shows the same steps with screenshots under Profile → Automatic Capture.

  1. In SPENT, open Profile → Automatic Capture and start the setup guide.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app, go to Automations and tap +.
  3. Scroll to the Wallet trigger, select the cards and categories you want to track, and tap Next.
  4. Add an action: type Spe and select Record Apple Pay Transaction.
  5. In the amount field choose Shortcut Input, tap it again and choose Amount. In the next field choose Shortcut Input again and choose Merchant.
  6. Tap the run button once to test. A SPENT window appears; tap Done. If iOS asks for permission to run SPENT, allow it.
  7. Save the automation, open it from the Automations list and set it to Run Immediately. The default, Run After Confirmation, will not capture purchases on its own. You can turn off Notify When Run so you are not notified on every payment.

What it captures and what it misses

Automatic capture records supported Apple Pay purchases on your iPhone. It is not a full bank sync, so plan to add some expenses by hand:

  • Cash and bank transfers: there is no Apple Pay event to capture.
  • Payments without a Wallet notification: for example a physical card swipe, or a card or bank that does not send transaction notifications to Wallet.
  • Missing details: if the amount does not arrive, SPENT marks the expense so you can fill it in. Refunds that arrive this way wait for your review.
  • Foreign currency: captured purchases are recorded in the capture currency you chose under Profile → Automatic Capture. Abroad, turn on Travel Mode and SPENT records in the local currency. You can fix the currency of any expense in History.

Captured expenses go to your personal city by default. If you share a city with a partner or roommates, you can choose to send captures there instead; see shared expense tracking for couples and roommates.

Check that it works

  1. Make a small Apple Pay purchase with a card you selected.
  2. Check that Wallet or your bank showed a transaction notification. Without one, no app can capture the purchase.
  3. Open History in SPENT and confirm that the amount, merchant and category are right, and that the purchase appears once.
  4. If nothing arrived, open the automation in Shortcuts and make sure it is set to Run Immediately, then add the purchase by hand.

Other ways to track Apple Pay spending

  • Wallet's own transaction list: some cards show recent purchases in Wallet. How much history you see depends on the card issuer.
  • Shortcuts with Notes or Numbers: the same Wallet trigger can append each purchase to a note or a spreadsheet, with no extra app. You get a log, but no categories, budgets or summaries.
  • Bank-linked budgeting apps: they import every transaction, including cash withdrawals and transfers, but you have to give them access to your bank.

SPENT sits between these: purchases are captured automatically, nothing is linked to your bank, and each expense goes into budgets, monthly summaries and a 3D city that shows where the month went. It also runs on Android, where it can record purchases from Google Wallet and card notifications.

Questions about automatic Apple Pay tracking

Can I track Apple Pay spending without connecting my bank?

Yes. A Shortcuts automation on your iPhone passes the amount and merchant of each supported Apple Pay purchase to SPENT. SPENT never asks for bank login details and does not read your bank account.

Which iOS version do I need?

The guided setup in SPENT needs iOS 27 or later. On earlier versions SPENT offers a manual setup option, but transaction details may be incomplete, so you may need to fill some in yourself.

Will every purchase be captured?

No. Only Apple Pay purchases that produce a transaction notification in Wallet can be captured, and that depends on your card and bank. Cash, bank transfers and other payments need to be added by hand.

Is SPENT free?

Yes, SPENT is free to download on the App Store and Google Play.

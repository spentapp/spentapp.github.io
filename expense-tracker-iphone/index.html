Expense tracker for iPhone without a bank connection

An iPhone expense tracker without bank linking: learn how SPENT records purchases, uses Apple Pay automation and turns spending categories into a city.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

How SPENT tracks spending

SPENT is an expense tracker for iPhone with English and Hebrew interfaces. It turns spending into a 3D city that changes during the month. Categories give you a visual way to see where your money goes, alongside expense history and monthly summaries. You can record income and create custom categories. Planning the month itself is covered in monthly budget app for iPhone.

Manual entry and optional Apple Pay automation

You can add purchases manually. For supported Apple Pay transactions, you can set up a personal automation in Apple Shortcuts on your own iPhone. Setup is required; installing SPENT alone does not enable automatic capture. Availability depends on your card and the transaction automation supported by Apple Wallet. This is not a bank feed and does not import every transaction from your bank.

What “no bank connection” means

SPENT does not ask you to link your bank account. Cash, transfers and purchases that the automation does not capture still need recording. Check your expense history against your actual purchases to spot gaps or duplicates. For foreign-currency purchases, review the amount and currency: automatic Apple Pay capture uses the main currency, while foreign-currency expenses can be entered manually.

Is it a fit for you?

SPENT may suit you if you want visual spending categories and budgeting on an iPhone without sharing bank login details. It is less suitable if you need a live bank balance or automatic reconciliation of every account. The iPhone app requires iOS 17 or later and is free to download. Check the App Store for current availability and pricing. If you want to keep a record with a partner, see shared expense tracking for couples.

Your first week

Start with your main currency and a few categories you actually use. Add your purchases, then review the history at the end of the week. If you enable automation, test a supported purchase and confirm the amount and category. The city helps you notice patterns; it does not replace checking the underlying records.

Questions about tracking without a bank connection

Does SPENT connect to my bank account?

No. SPENT does not ask for bank login details and does not read your bank account. You record purchases yourself, or let a personal Apple Pay automation add supported ones.

Does SPENT capture Apple Pay purchases automatically?

Only after you set up a personal automation in the Shortcuts app on your iPhone. Availability depends on your card and on the transaction automation Apple Wallet supports. Cash and bank transfers still need to be added by hand.

Is SPENT free, and which iPhones does it run on?

SPENT is free to download and needs iOS 17 or later. It is available in English and Hebrew.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. SPENT is also on Google Play for Android 8 or later. This guide covers the iPhone app.

Expense tracker for iPhone without a bank connection

An iPhone expense tracker without bank linking: learn how SPENT records purchases, uses Apple Pay automation and turns spending categories into a city.

Download on the App Store

How SPENT tracks spending

SPENT is an expense tracker for iPhone with English and Hebrew interfaces. It turns spending into a 3D city that changes during the month. Categories give you a visual way to see where your money goes, alongside expense history and monthly summaries. You can record income, set a monthly budget and create custom categories.

Manual entry and optional Apple Pay automation

You can add purchases manually. For supported Apple Pay transactions, you can set up a personal automation in Apple Shortcuts on your own iPhone. Setup is required; installing SPENT alone does not enable automatic capture. Availability depends on your card and the transaction automation supported by Apple Wallet. This is not a bank feed and does not import every transaction from your bank.

What “no bank connection” means

SPENT does not ask you to link your bank account. Cash, transfers and purchases that the automation does not capture still need recording. Check your expense history against your actual purchases to spot gaps or duplicates. For foreign-currency purchases, review the amount and currency: automatic Apple Pay capture uses the main currency, while foreign-currency expenses can be entered manually.

Is it a fit for you?

SPENT may suit you if you want visual spending categories and budgeting on an iPhone without sharing bank login details. It is less suitable if you need a live bank balance, automatic reconciliation of every account or an Android app. The iPhone app requires iOS 17 or later and is free to download. Check the App Store for current availability and pricing.

Your first week

Start with your main currency and a few categories you actually use. Add your purchases, then review the history at the end of the week. If you enable automation, test a supported purchase and confirm the amount and category. The city helps you notice patterns; it does not replace checking the underlying records.

Inside the app

What is SPENT?

SPENT is a free expense tracker and budget app for iPhone that turns your monthly spending into a 3D city. Each purchase adds to a district: food, shopping, transport, home and more. By the end of the month the city shows where your money went, without charts you need to read.

SPENT works without connecting a bank account. You add an expense in a few taps, or let Apple Pay purchases come in through a one-time Shortcuts automation. Your personal data is stored on your iPhone and backed up to your own iCloud, and the app has no ads and no tracking.

Who it is for

  • People who want to track spending without linking a bank account.
  • Anyone who finds spreadsheets and budget charts hard to keep up with.
  • Couples, roommates and families who want one shared budget, with a shared city.
  • Travellers who spend in more than one currency (Travel Mode).

Good to know

  • SPENT is for iPhone (iOS 17 or later). There is no Android version on Google Play yet.
  • Automatic capture of Apple Pay purchases needs a one-time Shortcuts automation. Captured purchases are recorded in your main currency; you can add foreign-currency expenses manually.
  • Shared cities need Sign in with Apple and an internet connection to sync.
  • SPENT is not a bank and does not give financial advice.

Questions

Does SPENT connect to my bank account?

No. SPENT has no bank connection. You enter expenses yourself, or capture Apple Pay purchases through a Shortcuts automation that you set up on your own iPhone.

What is the best way to track expenses on iPhone without a bank connection?

With SPENT you log each purchase yourself in a few seconds, or let Apple Pay purchases arrive automatically through a Shortcuts automation. Nothing is pulled from your bank, so you don't share login details with anyone.

How is SPENT different from other budget apps?

Most budget apps show tables and charts. SPENT shows your month as a living 3D city, so you see at a glance which part of your life costs the most. It also has budgets, savings goals, income, recurring expenses and a summary at the end of every month.

Is SPENT free?

SPENT is free to download from the App Store.

Is my financial data private?

Your personal expenses, budgets and city are stored on your iPhone and backed up to your own iCloud. SPENT has no ads, no tracking libraries and does not sell or share your financial data. Shared cities are stored on a secure server and only the members of that city can see them.

Can I set a monthly budget and savings goals?

Yes. You can set a monthly budget, a budget per category, savings goals with deposits, income and recurring expenses.

Can I use SPENT with my partner?

Yes. A shared city lets two or more people add expenses to one city, see who paid, and follow one budget. Every member has equal rights.

Does SPENT support foreign currencies?

Yes. Each city has a main currency, and Travel Mode suggests the local currency when you are abroad. Automatic Apple Pay capture is recorded in your main currency, and foreign-currency expenses can be added by hand.

Does SPENT work outside Israel?

The app is available on the App Store in other countries, including the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and it works in English. Manual entry works anywhere. Automatic capture depends on whether Apple Wallet's transaction automation is available for your card.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. SPENT is available for iPhone on the App Store.

Who makes SPENT?

SPENT is built by Binyamin Wisemon, an independent developer. Questions and feedback: spentapp.support@gmail.com.

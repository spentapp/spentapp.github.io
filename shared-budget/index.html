Shared expense tracking for couples and roommates

Track shared purchases in SPENT on iPhone, see who paid and review a shared budget with a partner, roommates or friends.

Download on the App Store

One place for shared purchases

SPENT lets a partner, roommates or friends add expenses to a shared city. Members can see who paid and follow the same budget. The city provides a visual overview of spending categories, while the expense records hold the details.

Agree what belongs in the shared city

Before adding purchases, decide which expenses you want to track together. For example, you might include groceries and household bills while keeping individual shopping in personal cities. Agree how to label categories and which date to use for a purchase so the monthly review stays understandable.

Keep the record useful

For each shared purchase, check the amount, category and payer. If two people might enter the same receipt, decide who records it and review the history for duplicates. Recording an expense does not itself transfer money between people.

Access and privacy

Shared cities require Sign in with Apple and an internet connection for synchronization. Their data is stored on a server for members to access. Members have equal rights, including updating the budget and city details and inviting or removing members. Read the privacy policy before sharing financial information. A separate friends feature can share a visual view of your personal city; that is different from a shared expense record.

A regular review together

Choose a time to review shared purchases and compare category totals with your plan. Correct missing entries before deciding whether to change the budget. SPENT runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later, in English and Hebrew. It does not require connecting a bank account. Each person should check current device compatibility in the App Store.
